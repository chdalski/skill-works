# D22: The rules of step 5a come in one commit at the end

Status: accepted

## Decision

The rules of step 5a come as one commit at the end of step 5, when all skills they name exist. Until then they do not apply in sessions in skill-works.

The rules of step 5a are the rules of the seed of skill-works: `simplicity`, `no-silent-target-weakening`, `claude-code-guide`, and the rule for instruction files ([D18](D18-instruction-guideline-in-the-workshop-set.md)).
