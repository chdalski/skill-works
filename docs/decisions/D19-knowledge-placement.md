# D19: One place for each piece of knowledge

Status: accepted

## Decision

Knowledge has exactly one place. The place depends on when the knowledge is needed:

- only while a skill works: in the references of its set;
- in every session: as a short rule in the seed;
- facts about the repository: in its `CLAUDE.md`.

## Consequences

- For skill-works, this is recorded in `.claude/rules/knowledge-placement.md`.
