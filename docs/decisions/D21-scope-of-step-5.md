# D21: Scope of step 5

Status: accepted

## Decision

Step 5 of the plan expands the workshop set and writes the seed of skill-works by hand, in the sub-steps 5a to 5e:

- 5a: the rules in the seed of skill-works;
- 5b: a shared place for the instruction guideline;
- 5c: the skills that design, write, and review skills;
- 5d: `sw-seed-audit`;
- 5e: the test bench skill.

There is no second set only for a trial. Step 6 shows two sets linked through one seed, with the maintenance set. Step 7 tests the seed generator on the setup of skill-works: the generator generates that setup, and if the result matches the hand-kept setup, skill-works becomes its own seed.
